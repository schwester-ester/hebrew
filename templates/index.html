First-time setup & connection help

In Anki desktop, open Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, enter 2055492159, and restart Anki. Keep Anki open, click Connect & sync, and approve this website’s request in Anki. If your browser asks to access devices on your local network, choose Allow.
